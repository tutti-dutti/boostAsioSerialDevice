## PART 1: SUPERCARS

### 1. All three Lambchops
All three (Chupacabra, Minotaur, Bonehorn) look flat and roofless from the side, like slabs with an open top.
- All: a low, closed, smoothly sloping roof with dark windows (not open-top), a sharp pointed nose instead of a flat front slab, and hexagon-shaped headlights, side intakes, and taillights.
- Keep the Chupacabra's rear view (Y-shaped taillights, black rear grille, twin exhausts), since it looks great.
- **Lambchop Chupacabra** (Aventador style): orange. A long sharp wedge, the roof peaking just behind the windshield, a medium rear wing.
- **Lambchop Minotaur** (Revuelto style): red. The biggest and meanest: long, wide, very low, Y-shaped headlights, a big rear wing, an exposed engine cover behind the cabin.
- **Lambchop Bonehorn** (Temerario style): green. Shorter and smoother than the others, with a smaller wing.
- They should look like one family but clearly different.

### 2. Shovel-A Thresher (C8 Corvette Stingray)
It has a long hood with the cabin pushed back, like an old front-engine Corvette. Move the cabin forward: a short, low front nose and a long rear deck behind the seats where the engine is. Big side air intakes in front of the rear wheels. Keep the red paint and sharp creases.

### 3. Porch Deathstalker (911 Turbo S)
Too boxy, like a sedan. Give it a smooth fastback roof that curves in one line from the top of the windshield down to the rear bumper. Round frog-eye headlights on top of the front fenders, wide rounded rear hips over the back wheels. Keep the ducktail spoiler and silver paint.

### 4. McRoar Great White (Speedtail)
Looks like a toothpick: too long and too skinny. Make it about 20% shorter and about 15% wider. Keep the bubble cabin, pointy nose, and tapered tail. Make the wheels a bit bigger so they fill the wheel arches.

## PART 2: TRUCKS

### 5. Voltra Ankylosaur (Cybertruck)
Looks like a dustpan or bathtub: flat separate panels stick up on the sides with an open top. Rebuild it as ONE solid closed body shaped like a doorstop from the side:
- A sharp peak at the top of the roof above the doors
- One straight flat slope from the peak down to the front bumper
- One straight flat slope from the peak down to the back
- Flat steel sides with no gaps, a dark windshield on the front slope
- A thin white light bar across the full front, a red one across the full back
- Silver steel, wheels inside angular wheel arches

### 6. River Run Swamp Beast (Rivian R1T)
Looks like a 1930s hot rod with round tube fenders. Make it a modern electric pickup:
- A smooth, clean, rounded-box body. Fenders blend into the body (no separate tubes).
- Two tall vertical oval "stadium" headlights at the front corners, connected by a thin glowing light bar across the front
- A smooth flat front with no grille (electric)
- A low, sleek cab with a sloped windshield, a short bed, and a thin light bar across the back
- Dark blue-gray paint, big modern wheels flush with the body

### 7. Coyota Tasmanian Devil (Tacoma TRD Pro)
Looks like the Thunder Bison with green paint. Make it clearly smaller (a mid-size truck): a shorter cab, a shorter bed, a lower roof, a narrower body, a black honeycomb grille, and a roof light bar.

### 8. Headbutt Mammoth (Ram Power Wagon)
Has a Jeep slotted grille. Give it a Ram crosshair grille (a big rectangle with a cross in it), a front winch, and make it taller and heavier than the Thunder Bison.

### 9. Bummer War Hippo (Hummer H1)
Looks great, just make it wider: the widest vehicle in the game, with a low, flat roof.

## PART 3: STREET AND WORLD BUGS

### 10. Spawn point
Every car spawns on top of the same crosswalk. Move the spawn point to a clear spot on a straight road, away from crosswalks and intersections. When I switch cars in the Garage, swap the new car in right where my current car is.

### 11. Crosswalks
Crosswalks float above the road and stick out past the road edges into the grass. Lay the stripes flat on the road surface, only as wide as the road.

### 12. Curbs and sidewalks
Curbs and sidewalks have gaps and floating pieces. Make them one continuous, smooth strip along the road, sitting on the ground.

### 13. Solid objects
I drove straight through a giant boulder. Make every big boulder, tree, building, pillar, and sign solid, with collision that matches its size and shape. Only small rocks and bushes can be driven over. Test by driving into a giant boulder at low and high speed in a truck and a supercar.

### 14. Surface label
The surface label in the top-left sometimes says "Sand" when I'm on grass. Make it show the correct surface.
